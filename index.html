Guide

Tools

SelectTap an annotation to move, recolour, resize, or delete it. Drag its handles to reshape.
TextPlaces a text label. Tap to type; empty labels are discarded.
DrawFreehand ink for circling or sketching.
LengthDrag a measured line. Short bars mark each end. Enter a value and pick a unit (mm, m, in, ft) — the number rides alongside the line.
MeasureAuto-measures real size from a calibrated camera. Set a scale once (pick a camera + enter distance), then draw a line for length or an area for a polygon — the value is computed for you. Double-tap a measurement to change its unit.
Calibrate cameraIn Measure, open Cameras… to teach the tool your camera. Photograph an object of known width at several known distances, mark it on each, and save. Reuse the saved camera on any photo — no ruler needed in the shot.
NoteA leader line from a point to a text label. The line meets the label on its near side, so it always points cleanly at the target.
PolygonTap to drop corners; use Close shape to finish. Shade an area or ring a region.
AngleDraw one side from the corner, then the second. The measured angle is written in the arc.
NumberDrops auto-incrementing markers for a legend or defect schedule. Set a prefix (e.g. B) and the counter continues B1, B2, B3. Clear all resets the count.
DefectTags in a condition-state colour. Tap the tool to re-use the last defect, double-tap for the full list, hold it for your recent five. In the list, tap a tile to place at the shown CS, or hold a tile to pick CS1–CS4 and favourite it.
QRDrops a scannable code onto the photo — enter a link, then tap to place. Uses the current ink colour and the S/M/L size. Double-tap to change the link. Pale colours are drawn in black so the code still scans.

Choosing defects

The tool buttonTap it to re-arm the last defect and place straight away. Double-tap to open the list. Hold it for the five most recent, each at the condition you used it at.
The listThree tabs — Defects, Tags, Elements. On Defects, the chips filter by material; ★ Favs gathers your favourites. Tap a tile to arm it; hold a tile to pick a condition and place in one gesture, or to make it a favourite.
Search and addThe one field does both. Typing filters the tiles across every material; Add lights up when what you typed matches nothing, and keeps it for the rest of the job. Tags and elements work the same way.
ConditionThe pill in the list sets the condition for the next defect. Tap it to step through Current, CS1, CS2, CS3, CS4; hold it to pick one directly. The coloured chip in the toolbar does the same for the defect already armed.
Ink and conditionFor a defect, the ink swatches are the condition: green CS1, yellow CS2, orange CS3, red CS4. It applies to the selected defect, or to the armed one when nothing is selected. White, black and blue set CS1. Other marks — notes, arrows, text — take the colour and nothing more.

Options

FillOn the text-bearing tools, toggles the solid backing behind text. Off swaps it for a crisp outline so the photo shows through while staying readable.
Colour · CurrentIn Defect, "Current" keeps the swatch you already picked instead of forcing the severity colour.
SketchAdds a blank landscape sheet to the album — punched like a page from a sketch pad — to draw on with the normal tools. Included in the report.
DividerDrops a straight rule across the photo — handy for splitting a composite shot.
Clear allRemoves every annotation on the current photo only. Undoable.
Start overWipes all photos and annotations and returns to a blank session.

Report

What fills the tableEvery defect placed from the Defects tab becomes one row. Tags, elements, measurements, notes and number badges near it fill that row's columns. Freehand marks and dividers stay decoration. Rows can also be typed by hand for things observed but not photographed.
Review before printingFill in the header, caption each photo, then correct any cell by typing in it. Untick a row or a photo to leave it out. Edits are remembered; Reset edits rebuilds the table from the photos.
Print / PDFBuilds a cover page, photo sheets and a defect schedule, then opens the print dialog — choose "Save as PDF". Photos are reduced for printing; the normal export is untouched.

Getting around

Zoom & panPinch or scroll to zoom; drag with two fingers, or use the +, fit, − buttons. Fit re-centres the photo.
PasteAdds an image copied to the clipboard as a new photo. Press Ctrl/Cmd+V, or use the Paste button. Phones will ask for clipboard permission the first time.
PhotosThumbnails along the bottom switch between loaded photos. Everything is saved on this device automatically — nothing is uploaded.
